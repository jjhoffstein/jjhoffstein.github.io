Privacy

This portfolio does not use advertising, analytics, or marketing cookies.

Local browser storage

The Alpha Site Scorer saves site entries in your browser so the dashboard can work between visits. The Snake game saves its high score in your browser. This information stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

Third-party services

The site loads its display font from Google Fonts. The Alpha Site Scorer loads mapping tiles from OpenStreetMap when you open its map view, and it loads its chart and map libraries from public CDNs. These providers may receive standard technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver their services.

Contact

If you email or call JJ Hoffstein, the information you provide is used to respond to your message.
