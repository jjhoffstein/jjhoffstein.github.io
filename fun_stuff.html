Fun Stuff

Being on a team isn't just about work - it's also about having fun.

One of my favorite quotes comes from comedian Charlie Chaplin: "A day without laughter is a day wasted."

Every time a team comes together, there is an opportunity for fun to be had.

With that in mind, View Fun Stuff Ideas is a document where I keep ideas for fun activities to do with teams.

If you have any ideas of your own, or if there are any you think I missed, please don't hesitate to let me know, I'd love to add to my ideas list!
